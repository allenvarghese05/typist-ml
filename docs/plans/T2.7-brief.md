# T2.7 brief — Retest composer (form B)

*Analyst brief. Facts only; no design or implementation.*

## 1. Task

- **ID:** T2.7 (milestone **M2 "Loop with heuristic and control"**, design doc §16; M2 "Done when": "A test participant goes baseline, assign, practice, retest end to end").
- **Title (verbatim, §16):** "Retest composer (form B)".
- **Acceptance criteria (verbatim, §16):** "At least 40 attempts per tracked bigram; different words from form A where possible".

## 2. Why it matters

`test_composer.py` (`services/api/src/typist/services/test_composer.py`, §7: `test_composer.py # baseline and retest texts`) currently composes only the shared baseline (form A) passages (T1.8). T2.7 adds the retest (form B) composer and persistence path:

- **Provides the post-practice measurement instrument for primary and secondary outcomes (§3.2, §3.5, §15.1, D05).** Both the primary outcome (per-set pooled error-rate change, retest minus baseline, minus control's change) and the key secondary outcome (per-set median IKI change, retest minus baseline, minus control's change) compare a participant's performance on their frozen assigned bigrams (`bigram_assignment`, 16 or 12 bigrams in 4-set mode; 8 or 6 in 2-set mode) during the two retest sessions (form B) against their two baseline sessions (form A).
- **Guarantees statistical power and avoids the `< 20 retest attempts` exclusion (§3.2, §3.5, §15.1, D23).** §3.5 notes: *"With about 200 attempts per set at retest, a set may show only around 10 errors, so error-rate changes will be noisy. That is why timing (IKI) is the key secondary outcome and why the retest guarantees at least 40 attempts per tracked bigram."* Furthermore, §15.1 / D23 excludes any assigned bigram with `< 20` retest attempts from both primary and secondary outcomes. Composing form B with at least 40 occurrences per tracked bigram gives a ≥ 2× safety buffer against broken attribution contexts (mistypes/backspaces at `j-1`).
- **Prevents word-memorisation confounds ("parallel test text (form B) ... different words from form A where possible", §3.2, §16).** If form B reused the exact words of form A, a participant could appear faster or more accurate at retest merely from memorising form A's word sequences, or could transfer word-specific familiarity rather than bigram-level motor learning.
- **Unblocks retest session planning (`session_planner.py`, T2.6), state transitions (T2.8), and the end-to-end M2 loop (T2.10).** `session_planner.plan_session` (built in T2.6) already queries `TextPassage` with `kind == "retest", form == "B", part == completed_count, participant_id == participant_id` and raises `PassageNotFoundError` when those rows are absent.

What breaks if it is wrong:
- **Under-coverage (< 40 occurrences of a tracked bigram across form B's parts):** fails the primary acceptance criterion, reduces per-set attempts below the ~160–200 per set assumed in §3.5, and risks dropping bigrams under the `< 20` retest-attempts exclusion rule (§15.1).
- **Word overlap with form A when non-form-A words were available:** fails `"different words from form A where possible"` and confounds retest outcomes with form-A word repetition.
- **Wrong `participant_id` or `kind`/`form`/`part` values on `text_passages`:** §8.1 states for `text_passages.participant_id`: *"Null for shared baseline texts; set for retest texts composed after freezing"*, and `session_planner.py` lines 341–350 explicitly filter retest passages by `kind == "retest"`, `form == "B"`, `part == completed_count`, and `participant_id == participant_id`. Leaving `participant_id = None` would cause `plan_session(..., phase="retest")` to raise `PassageNotFoundError`.
- **Overwriting a form B passage already referenced by a `session_blocks` row:** breaks reproducibility (`session_blocks.passage_id` FK and frozen test text rule in §3.2 / §16 Pilot runbook).

## 3. Governing decisions

- **D01 (Experiment unit — within-person):** each participant has their own frozen `bigram_assignment` rows (16 or 12 bigrams across 4 sets, or 8/6 across 2 sets) and their own per-participant `text_passages` rows for `kind="retest", form="B"` (§8.1).
- **D04 (Assignment frozen before practice):** §8.1 notes for `text_passages.participant_id`: *"Null for shared baseline texts; set for retest texts composed after freezing"*. Form B is composed for a participant after their `bigram_assignment` rows are frozen.
- **D05 (Outcomes):** primary and secondary outcomes pool over each set's assigned bigrams across the two retest sessions vs the two baseline sessions.
- **D08 (Backend stack):** Python 3.12, FastAPI, SQLModel, Alembic, SQLite in WAL mode with `PRAGMA foreign_keys=ON`, Typer CLI.
- **D13 (Text scope):** lowercase `a` to `z` and single spaces only; letter-letter bigrams within words only (`count_bigrams(text)` in `corpus/word_index.py`).
- **D17 (Word list):** `wordfreq` English word list (`data/corpus/words.json`, top 20,000 pure `a–z` words built by `typist corpus build`).
- **D23 (Analysis plan definitions):** bigrams with `< 20` pooled retest attempts are excluded from outcomes; abandoned retest sessions are excluded from outcomes.
- **§17.1 Engineering standards:** *"Every experiment parameter lives in config, never as a literal inside logic."* *"Randomness always goes through a seeded generator passed in from the caller."*

## 5. Inputs and outputs

**Inputs:**
- `participant_id: str` (identifying the participant whose retest form B is being composed).
- The participant's frozen `bigram_assignment` rows in SQLite (or the tracked bigram set — see Open Question 1).
- The existing shared baseline (form A) `TextPassage` rows (`kind="baseline", form="A", participant_id=None`), from which the set of form-A words is extracted (`set(word for passage in form_a_passages for word in passage.text.split(" "))`) so form B can avoid them where possible.
- `WordIndex` loaded from `Settings.corpus_dir` (`words.json`, `top_bigrams.json`).
- Retest configuration parameters from `experiment_config` (see Open Question 2).
- Seeded `random.Random` instance passed in by the caller (e.g. seeded from `f"{corpus_seed}:{participant_id}:B"` or `f"{assignment_seed}:{participant_id}"` — see Open Question 4).

**Outputs:**
- Two per-participant `TextPassage` rows (`kind="retest"`, `form="B"`, `part=0` and `part=1`, `participant_id=participant_id`, `text`, `bigram_counts=count_bigrams(text)`), persisted to `text_passages` in SQLite.
- Retest composition and persistence functions in `services/api/src/typist/services/test_composer.py` (and any CLI / assignment / session-planner hook — see Open Question 5).
- If new `experiment_config` keys are added: a new data-only Alembic migration `0008_retest_config.py` and updated test pin constants (`HEAD`, `SEEDED_CONFIG_ROWS`, `EXPECTED_CONFIG`).
- Unit and integration tests proving:
  1. Combined occurrences across the two form B passages is `>= 40` for every target/tracked bigram, and `bigram_counts` matches an independent within-word letter-letter recount.
  2. Form B uses different words from form A where possible (zero overlap with form A words when the non-form-A vocabulary suffices, and graceful fallback only when a bigram's non-form-A words are exhausted).
  3. Determinism (same seed + inputs → identical form B texts) and idempotency / in-use freeze guard on persistence.

## 6. Constraints

- **Coverage acceptance criterion (§3.2, §3.5, §16):** across the two form B passages (`part=0` and `part=1`) combined, every target bigram for the retest must occur at least 40 times (`sum(p.bigram_counts.get(b, 0) for p in passages) >= 40`).
- **Word-disjointness acceptance criterion (§3.2, §16):** `"different words from form A where possible"`. Words that appeared in any shared form A passage (`kind="baseline", form="A", participant_id=None`) must be excluded from candidate selection whenever non-form-A words in the corpus can still cover the bigram and respect the per-part word-repetition limit (`_MAX_USES_PER_PART = 2`), falling back to form-A words only if a bigram has no remaining eligible non-form-A words.
- **Per-participant rows in `text_passages` (§8.1, `models/text_passages.py`, `session_planner.py`):** form B rows must have `kind="retest"`, `form="B"`, `part=0..parts-1`, and `participant_id=<participant_id>`, matching the partial unique index `uq_text_passages_participant_kind_form_part` and `session_planner.plan_session`.
- **Charset and word rules (D13, §13.1, §13.3, T1.8 owner answer 8):** lowercase `a–z` words of length 2–10 (`_MIN_WORD_LEN = 2`, `_MAX_WORD_LEN = 10`) from `WordIndex.words`, joined by single spaces, no leading/trailing spaces, each word used at most twice per part (`_MAX_USES_PER_PART = 2`), `bigram_counts` computed by `count_bigrams(text)` (within-word letter-letter only).
- **Determinism and seeded RNG (CLAUDE.md, §13.1, §17.1):** randomness comes exclusively from a `random.Random` instance passed in by the caller.
- **Freeze / in-use guard (§3.2, §16 Pilot runbook, T1.8 owner answer 7):** once any `session_blocks` row references one of the participant's form B passages, replacing those passages must be refused.
- **Platform & CI:** pure Python + SQLite; all tests must run and pass on Linux CI without `mlx-lm`.

## 7. Edge cases

1. **What "per tracked bigram" means in §3.2 / §3.5 / §16 ( participant's frozen assigned bigrams vs all 150 corpus tracked bigrams ).**
   - In **§3.5** (page 8), the design doc explains the 40-attempt number: *"With about 200 attempts per set at retest, a set may show only around 10 errors, so error-rate changes will be noisy. That is why timing (IKI) is the key secondary outcome and why the retest guarantees at least 40 attempts per tracked bigram."* (4 bigrams per set × 40 attempts = 160–200 attempts per set).
   - In **§8.1** (`text_passages.participant_id`, page 17), the design doc says: *"Null for shared baseline texts; set for retest texts composed after freezing"*. The reason retest texts are per-participant and composed *after freezing* `bigram_assignment` is that each participant's retest targets their frozen assigned bigrams (16 or 12 bigrams in 4-set mode; 8 or 6 in 2-set mode).
   - Conversely, in `corpus/word_index.py` and `0002_baseline_config`, `tracked_bigrams` / `tracked_bigram_count = 150` is the name given to the 150 corpus bigrams, and feasibility mathematics differs sharply between the two interpretations:
     - Covering a participant's **16 frozen assigned bigrams** at `>= 40` occurrences across 2 parts of ~2,700 chars requires only `16 × 40 = 640` target-bigram hits inside ~5,400 chars (~1,000 words), which is easily achieved within the ~10-minute session length (§3.2) even when every word is used at most twice per part and all form-A words (~850 unique words) are excluded.
     - Covering **all 150 corpus bigrams** at `>= 40` occurrences across 2 parts while excluding form-A words and capping word reuse at 2 per part: rare bigrams in the top 150 (such as `uc`, `ey`, `wk`, `gr`) have fewer than ~30 words of length 2–10 in the 20,000-word list, and even if achievable, packing `150 × 40 = 6,000` target hits requires ~6,500–7,500 chars per part (~25–30 minutes per session instead of §3.2's "About 10 minutes each"), **or** composing for the participant's assigned bigrams first and filling the rest of the 2,700-char part budget with the remaining top-150 bigrams.
   - See **Open Question 1**.
2. **Vocabulary exhaustion when excluding form A words for a rare bigram.**
   - Suppose form A used ~850 distinct words across its two 2,700-char parts. If a target bigram has only a small number of containing words in the 20,000-word list (or if many of its highest-scoring words were used in form A), strictly banning form-A words could leave fewer than `ceil(40 / (2 * 2)) = 10` words for that bigram across 2 parts (since each word can be used at most twice per part = 4 times across 2 parts, or more if a word contains the bigram twice).
   - Concrete example: if bigram `xyz` only appears in 8 non-form-A words (max 32 occurrences across 2 parts at 2 uses/part), strict exclusion cannot reach 40 unless the composer falls back to allowing form-A words once non-form-A words for that bigram are exhausted (matching the acceptance phrasing `"different words from form A where possible"`).
3. **`_CANDIDATE_POOL = 400` slicing before vs after excluding form A words.**
   - In `test_composer.py` line 93, `_eligible(words, used)` takes the first `_CANDIDATE_POOL = 400` words from `by_target[source]` that have `used.get(word, 0) < _MAX_USES_PER_PART`. If non-form-A filtering happens *preferentially* (preferring eligible words not in `excluded_words`, and only falling back to `excluded_words` if no non-excluded word is eligible for `source`), `by_target[source]` must be filtered to non-excluded words *before* taking the first `_CANDIDATE_POOL` candidates, otherwise form-A words at the top of the frequency list would consume slots in `_CANDIDATE_POOL` or be picked when non-form-A words exist further down the list.
4. **Form A passages missing when composing form B.**
   - If someone calls the retest composer (or CLI command) before `compose-baseline` has been run, what is the excluded word set? Should the DB/CLI helper require shared form A passages to exist in `text_passages` (or derive form A words on the fly / raise an error)? (See Open Question 3.)
5. **Participant has no frozen `bigram_assignment` rows yet.**
   - Because §8.1 specifies retest texts are *"composed after freezing"*, calling retest composition for a participant who has not yet been assigned (`bigram_assignment` empty) must raise a clear typed error (e.g. `ParticipantNotAssignedError` / `RetestConfigurationError`).
6. **Idempotency and `--replace` freeze guard per participant.**
   - Unlike baseline (`participant_id IS NULL`), `text_passages` for retest are scoped to `participant_id`. `persist_retest(session, participant_id, passages, replace=...)` must query and compare only `kind == "retest", form == "B", participant_id == participant_id`, and its `SessionBlock` reference check must check whether any `session_blocks.passage_id` references *this participant's* existing form B passages (not another participant's or baseline's).
7. **After all target bigrams reach `>= 40` occurrences before `retest_part_chars` is reached.**
   - If the target set is the participant's 16 (or 12 / 8 / 6) assigned bigrams, the composer will reach 40 occurrences per assigned bigram well before the 2 parts × 2,700 chars budget is full. Once all assigned bigrams have reached `target` (40), how should the composer fill the remainder of the part budget (e.g. continue cycling through the assigned bigrams / lowest-coverage tracked bigrams using non-form-A words)? (See Open Question 1 & 3.)

## 8. Risks

1. **Ambiguity between "participant's frozen assigned bigrams" vs "all 150 corpus tracked bigrams" for the ≥ 40 threshold (Open Question 1):** If tests and implementation disagree on which set of bigrams must have `>= 40` occurrences in form B, either the session length explodes to ~30 minutes per session (if all 150 must hit 40) or a test checking all 150 fails (if only the participant's assigned bigrams are targeted to 40).
2. **Accidental word overlap with form A:** If form-A words are only penalised in scoring rather than strictly excluded whenever non-form-A words are available for the source bigram, form B will unnecessarily reuse high-frequency form-A words, violating `"different words from form A where possible"`.
3. **Migration pin drift:** If T2.7 adds a new migration `0008_retest_config` for retest config keys, `HEAD`, `SEEDED_CONFIG_ROWS`, `PROBE_REBUILD_REVISION` in `tests/integration/test_migrations.py` and `EXPECTED_CONFIG` in `tests/integration/test_schema.py` must be updated in lockstep or CI will fail.
4. **Missing hook into the participant lifecycle:** `session_planner.plan_session` expects `TextPassage(kind="retest", form="B", participant_id=participant_id, part=0..1)` to already exist in `text_passages` when a retest session starts. If neither `assign` nor a CLI command / service hook persists form B passages, starting a retest session in M2 will fail with `PassageNotFoundError`.

## 9. Open questions

1. **Which bigrams must reach `>= 40` occurrences in form B ("tracked bigram" in §3.2 / §3.5 / §16)?**
   - **Option 1A (Participant's frozen assigned bigrams from `bigram_assignment`):** Form B is per-participant and *"composed after freezing"* (§8.1), and §3.5 states *"With about 200 attempts per set at retest ... that is why the retest guarantees at least 40 attempts per tracked bigram"* (4 bigrams/set × 40 = 160–200 attempts/set). Under 1A, every bigram in the participant's frozen `bigram_assignment` (16 or 12 in 4-set mode; 8 or 6 in 2-set mode) is guaranteed `>= 40` combined occurrences across the two 2,700-char form B parts (and once all assigned bigrams reach 40, the composer can either continue balancing the assigned bigrams or cover the rest of the 150 corpus bigrams until `part_chars` is reached).
   - **Option 1B (All 150 corpus `tracked_bigrams`):** Every one of the 150 bigrams in `top_bigrams.json` must reach `>= 40` occurrences across the two form B parts (which would require ~6,500–7,500 chars per part instead of 2,700 chars, or lifting the 2-uses-per-part word cap).

   - *Reasoning:*
     - `compose_retest(config, index, participant_id, assigned_bigrams, rng, *, excluded_words=())` splits each bigram's word list into `preferred` (words not in `excluded_words`) and `fallback` (words in `excluded_words`). At each step, `_eligible` / `_has_eligible` inspects `preferred[source]` first (taking up to `_CANDIDATE_POOL = 400` words with `used[w] < _MAX_USES_PER_PART`); it falls back to `fallback[source]` **only** when `preferred[source]` has zero eligible words left in the current part.
     - To distribute the 40 occurrences evenly across both retest sessions (`part = 0` and `part = 1`) rather than front-loading all 40 into part 0, part `p` first brings every assigned bigram up to a cumulative target of `math.ceil(config.retest_min_occurrences * (p + 1) / config.retest_parts)` (and `config.retest_min_occurrences` on the final part).
